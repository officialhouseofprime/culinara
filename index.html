Our Story

A problem hiding in plain sight

Some of the best cooking happening right now isn't happening in restaurants. It's happening in home kitchens, at pop-up dinners, in catering vans on the way to someone's backyard — made by chefs who never got a storefront, a review score, or a way for strangers to find them.

Meanwhile, the people looking for a private chef — for a birthday, an anniversary, a weekly meal plan, a wedding — are stuck asking friends for referrals or trusting a name on a flyer. There was no real, trustworthy place for the two to meet. That's the gap CULINARA exists to close.

We built CULINARA as a stage for independent chefs and a front door for the people looking for them: a place to post real work, build a real reputation, and get booked — directly, fairly, on their own terms. Take a look at chefs on the pass or read a few case studies to see it in action.

Our mission

Give every private chef a stage, and every host a way to find the right one — with nothing standing between them.

The Team

CULINARA is built by a small crew who've worked kitchen lines, run private events, and spent too many hours trying to find a chef through DMs and word-of-mouth. We're chefs, engineers, and people who just really care about a good table.

FAQs

Questions, answered

How does CULINARA work?

Chefs create a profile and post real photos and video of their work. Clients browse verified chef profiles and reach out directly to book — CULINARA never sits between you as a paid middleman.

Is CULINARA free to join?

Yes, creating a chef or client account is free. You only pay the chef directly for the work you book.

How are chefs verified?

Every chef application is reviewed by hand — identity, experience, and the work itself — before a profile goes live. See a sample of chefs on the pass.

What kinds of chefs are on CULINARA?

Independent private chefs covering everything from weekly meal prep to full event catering, across a wide range of cuisines — read a few case studies.

How quickly will I hear back after I apply or sign up?

Our team reviews every application by hand and responds within 2 business days.

Privacy Policy & Terms

This is placeholder legal copy for a landing page. Replace it with a policy reviewed by a lawyer before you launch.

1. What we collect

When you sign up, we collect the information you provide directly: name, email, phone number, and — for chef applicants — your CV/résumé, cover letter, and the answer to why you'd like to join CULINARA. Chefs who are approved may also upload photos, videos, and profile details for their public listing.

2. How we use it

We use your information to review applications, operate chef and client accounts, connect chefs with clients, and communicate with you about your account or bookings. We do not sell your personal information.

3. What's public

For approved chef profiles, your name, chef type, photos, videos, and portfolio content are shown publicly on CULINARA. Your CV, cover letter, and application answers are never made public — they're reviewed internally only.

4. Data storage & security

Uploaded files and account data are stored securely and retained only as long as needed to operate your account or as required by law. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Your choices

You can update your profile, withdraw an application, or delete your account at any time by contacting us at hello@culinara.com.

6. Terms of use

By joining CULINARA, chefs agree to provide accurate information and represent their own work. Clients agree to use the platform to make genuine booking inquiries. CULINARA facilitates introductions between chefs and clients; agreements about specific bookings, pricing, and service are made directly between the two parties.
