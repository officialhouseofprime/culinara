How payment settlement works right now

No live payment processor is connected yet (Stripe Connect isn't available to Kenya-based accounts directly, and M-Pesa Daraja requires a registered business Till). For now, clients and chefs settle payment directly (e.g. M-Pesa), and you mark each booking's payment status manually from the Bookings tab as money is confirmed.
